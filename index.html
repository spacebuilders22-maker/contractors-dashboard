    Model notes & assumptions

    Embedded cost model from contractor Quote #26072701 and local Miami rate audit spreadsheet (59 priced lines, plus the quote's $0 blueprint addenda SPA-01 / HTR-01 and an ENG-01 engineering placeholder). Quote/model numbers kept here for validation only.

    Baseline pool: 60×38 ft, water SF ≈ 1,580. Spa 7×7 separate. Scaled Quoted Direct ≈ $115,771 → quoted billed ≈ $152,330 (stacked fee ×1.3158, thickness 1.0, 9 lights, no owner-supply).

    Markup (quoted stacked): GC 5% as gross margin (Direct / 0.95) then Fee 20% as gross margin (/ 0.80) → effective multiplier ×1.3158. Fair models apply a single fee of 15% or 12% (or custom 10–35%) on direct.

    Shell thickness factor: multiplies shotcrete CY (06-010, spa shotcrete) and rebar qty (05-020, SPA-04). When factor > 1.0, forms LF get a small bump: ×(1 + 0.12×(factor−1)).

    Site work: excavation intensity multiplies Area 04-* lines only. Haul-off $/truck and truck-qty multiplier override 04-050 (quoted 17 truck × $200/truck). Gravel/backfill toggles zero those lines when off. Temp fence toggles 03-010.

    Depth / water SF: avg depth factor scales Area basis (volume proxy). Water SF override replaces auto water SF display and rescales Area vs baseline water SF when set > 0.

    Equipment configurator (E): every Equipment-KPI line has Quoted (Indigo) / Owner-supplied — MT Pool (where Stan has an invoice) / Local fair / retail (existing audit fair mid) / Custom $ (your $/ea or lump sum, saved in this browser) / Not included. Lights: qty (ea) × $/ea (default 9 ea × $600/ea; recommend 3 ea). Owner-supplied items stay in Direct at Stan's MT Pool cost (pump $1,491.30, filter $1,151.41, lights $435.67/ea, blower $240.42, pads + J-box $256.91); AUT-01 owner = $0 credit with Stan's AquaLink $4,238.83 shown separately (not in Direct). Custom fair = min(your price, fair mid). Salt and heater include toggles gate 11-030/08-020 and HTR-*/08-030.

    Fair mid: audit Fair Mid baselines × same scale factors; shotcrete fair $220/CY; forms fair $20/LF (quoted bars use your sliders). Fair $/unit is always shown in the quote's own unit for that line.

    Units: every line carries its unit of measure exactly as written in Indigo's Budget sheet — ls (lump sum), LF, sq ft, CY, truck (truckload), ea. Rebar is priced per LF ($0.60/LF), not per lb.

    Soft costs (F): each control is a lump-sum $ on a 1 ls line, defaulting to the quoted amount. Engineering plans → ENG-01 (default $0: the quote has no engineering line; the contract says engineering is included, i.e. bundled in 01-010; fair = quoted). Design / blueprints → 01-010 ($2,000; spa/heater addenda SPA-01 / HTR-01 are $0 in the quote). County fees, per permit: building 02-010 $2,000, plumbing 02-020 $0, electrical 02-030 $900, DERM/zoning 02-040 $0, permit runner 01-020 $0 (contract puts permit-running in General Conditions).

    Permits preset: Quoted uses line allowances; Actual Miami-Dade sets ~$800 building / ~$149 electrical (IO 4-63). Editing either fee shows “Custom (edited)”. Fair values for 01-/02- lines are unchanged audit numbers.
