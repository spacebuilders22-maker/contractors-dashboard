    Model notes & assumptions

    Embedded cost model from contractor Quote #26072701 and local Miami rate audit spreadsheet (59 line items). Quote/model numbers kept here for validation only.

    Baseline pool: 60×38 ft, water SF ≈ 1,580. Spa 7×7 separate. Scaled Quoted Direct ≈ $115,771 → quoted billed ≈ $152,330 (stacked fee ×1.3158, thickness 1.0, 9 lights, no owner-supply).

    Markup (quoted stacked): GC 5% as gross margin (Direct / 0.95) then Fee 20% as gross margin (/ 0.80) → effective multiplier ×1.3158. Fair models apply a single fee of 15% or 12% (or custom 10–35%) on direct.

    Shell thickness factor: multiplies shotcrete CY (06-010, spa shotcrete) and rebar qty (05-020, SPA-04). When factor > 1.0, forms LF get a small bump: ×(1 + 0.12×(factor−1)).

    Site work: excavation intensity multiplies Area 04-* lines only. Haul-off unit $/load and multiplier override 04-050. Gravel/backfill toggles zero those lines when off. Temp fence toggles 03-010.

    Depth / water SF: avg depth factor scales Area basis (volume proxy). Water SF override replaces auto water SF display and rescales Area vs baseline water SF when set > 0.

    Lights: quantity × unit $ (default 9 × $600). Recommend 3. Owner-supply master sets unit to $436.

    Owner-supply (Stan MT Pool): pump $1,491.30, filter $1,151.41, blower $240.42, AUT credit $0 (saves $2,800 quoted AquaLink).

    Fair mid: audit Fair Mid baselines × same scale factors; shotcrete fair $220/CY; forms fair $20/LF (quoted bars use your sliders).

    Permits: Quoted uses line allowances; Actual Miami-Dade uses published ~$800 building / ~$149 electrical.
