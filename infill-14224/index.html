    Florida Entry Builders proposal — exclusions & terms

    Fixed bid $53,578.79, proposal dated Oct 1, 2026. Items below are excluded from their price and would be extra (owner or other trades):

    • Demolition
    • Debris hauling
    • Footers and tie beams
    • Unsuitable soil removal
    • Utilities and drainage
    • A/E (architecture / engineering)
    • Survey and permits
    • All testing (incl. density / compaction tests)
    • Curing treatments
    • Saw-cut joints

    Warranty: 1-year workmanship warranty.

    No quantities: the proposal gives a lump sum only — no CY of fill, CY of concrete, SF of slab, or lift/compaction spec — so scope can't be checked line by line. Ask for quantities before comparing like-for-like.

    Sanity checks (defaults: 3 ft depth, 2,440 SF)

      Model notes & assumptions

      Areas: Bedroom wing 1,250 SF; Garage (converted block — Bedrooms 04–05, Bath 03, laundry) 590 SF; Kitchen + dining + front half of living room 600 SF = 2,440 SF. Custom row is off / 0 SF by default.

      Fill: compacted CY = SF × depth ft ÷ 27. Loose (delivered) CY = compacted × (1 + shrink %). Fill priced on loose CY at $30/CY delivered (Sunshine Coast Materials limerock fill, published price).

      Concrete: SF × thickness in ÷ 12 ÷ 27 + thickened edge LF × width × (edge depth − slab thickness) ÷ 27, then × (1 + waste %). Priced at $160/CY delivered (Miami range $136–220/CY). Pump is a lump sum.

      Vapor barrier + WWF mesh + chairs: SF × (1 + overlap %) × $/SF. Dowels + epoxy into existing slab/footings: lump sum.

      Labor: crew-day = people × $/hr × hr/day. Fill crew days auto = ⌈compacted CY ÷ CY/day⌉ (≈100 CY/day placed and compacted in lifts inside the house); untick auto to override. Compactor + small loader rental is charged per fill crew day. Slab crew = prep days (forming, steel, VB) + pour days.

      Density testing: auto = ⌈depth ft⌉ lifts × ⌈SF ÷ 1,000⌉ tests at ~$150 each. Owner-paid (Florida Entry excludes all testing), so no fee is applied to it.

      Fee: contractor overhead & profit % on direct cost (materials + labor + equipment). Cost-plus total = direct + fee (+ testing when included).

      Not included on either side: demo, haul-off, footers/tie beams, unsuitable soil, utilities, A/E, survey, permits, curing, saw-cutting. Chart.js loads from CDN (needs network once).
